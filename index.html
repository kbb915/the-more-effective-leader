The leadership challenge

Knowing What to Do Isn’t Always Enough.

Most experienced leaders understand the importance of communication, accountability, sound judgment, and leading by example. But knowing what effective leadership looks like doesn’t mean we always practice it.

Pressure can make us reactive. Disagreement can make us defensive. Uncertainty can cause hesitation. And our desire to get things done can lead us to take responsibility for things that belong to others.

These situations reveal patterns in how we function as leaders.

The More Effective Leader helps you recognize those patterns and intentionally develop better ways of leading.

A different approach

Don’t Just Learn More About Leadership. Change How You Lead.

Traditional leadership development often focuses on introducing new concepts, models, and skills.

Those things have value. But understanding leadership and functioning effectively as a leader aren’t always the same.

The More Effective Leader emphasizes putting what you learn into practice. Each week, participants apply meaningful leadership concepts to real situations, examine their responses, and practice becoming more effective in how they lead.

How the eight weeks work

Learn It. Apply It. Discuss It. Improve.

STEP 1

Learn.

Join a focused weekly online lesson exploring how effective leaders think, respond, and function.

STEP 2

Apply.

Put the week’s learning into practice through a real situation in your current leadership role.

STEP 3

Discuss.

Meet with a small peer cohort to discuss what you tried, what happened, and what you learned.

STEP 4

Reflect.

Identify what you did differently, the effect it had, and how you’ll continue improving.

There are no lengthy academic papers or assignments disconnected from your responsibilities. Your daily leadership work provides the opportunities to practice what you’re learning.

The curriculum

Week 1Understanding How You Lead+

Recognize how your assumptions, beliefs, and commitments influence your leadership behavior. Identify patterns in how you typically respond to difficult situations and where you want to become more effective.

Week 2Changing How You See Challenges+

Examine how your interpretation of circumstances influences your actions and results. Learn to recognize limiting perspectives and consider new ways of responding to familiar leadership challenges.

Week 3Leading Under Pressure+

Develop the ability to remain thoughtful and intentional when facing tension, disagreement, and uncertainty. Practice maintaining your commitments without becoming reactive, defensive, or avoidant.

Week 4Sustaining Your Effectiveness+

Recognize when your drive to achieve, tendency to overfunction, or habit of taking on too much responsibility begins to undermine your effectiveness. Identify changes that support healthier, more sustainable leadership.

Week 5Having Conversations That Matter+

Strengthen your ability to address difficult issues directly, give meaningful feedback, and maintain productive relationships even when conversations are uncomfortable.

Week 6Ownership and Accountability+

Recognize the difference between taking responsibility for your leadership and assuming responsibility for everyone else's work. Learn to establish clear expectations, encourage ownership, and address accountability without constant intervention.

Week 7Leading Through Change+

Develop the ability to lead constructively when circumstances shift, people resist, or expectations change. Practice engaging others while remaining clear about the outcomes you're committed to producing.

Week 8Leading Differently+

Evaluate how your leadership behavior has changed throughout the program. Identify what you're doing differently, assess its impact on your relationships and results, and establish a plan to sustain your progress.
