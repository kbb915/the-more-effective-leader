The work behind the work

Some challenges need a different way of leading.

You know your field. You care about your people and your work. Yet the same difficult patterns can keep showing up in your team.

This program creates space to examine your contribution, try a different response, and learn from what happens next.

  • 01How do I help people take ownership instead of bringing every decision back to me?
  • 02How can I address difficult issues without damaging working relationships?
  • 03What will help my team follow through when priorities compete?
  • 04How can I stay thoughtful and effective when the pressure rises?

What you’ll practice

More range. Better judgment.
Stronger leadership.

Eight areas of practice, connected to the situations you face as a leader.

01

Understand how you lead

Notice the assumptions and familiar responses that shape your decisions, conversations, and relationships.

02

Stay steady under pressure

Create room for a considered response when tension, uncertainty, or disagreement makes reacting easier.

03

Find a different way forward

Question approaches that have stopped helping and test new ways of moving important work ahead.

04

Build sustainable leadership habits

Recognize when taking on more is getting in the way of your effectiveness—and your team’s growth.

05

Have the conversations that matter

Practice direct, constructive conversations about expectations, feedback, and performance.

06

Exercise judgment in complexity

Work through competing needs and incomplete information without waiting for a perfect answer.

07

Help people move through change

Listen for what sits behind resistance and engage others in the work of moving forward.

08

Make learning part of how you work

Turn useful insights into repeatable actions, with a plan to continue after the program ends.

A practical weekly rhythm

Bring your work.
Leave ready to act.

Each week connects a live, facilitated conversation with practice in your own role. You’ll work alongside peers bringing different perspectives to the shared challenges of leading people, making decisions, and delivering results.

The weekly commitment is a 75-minute live session and a 45-minute cohort meeting. Live sessions are recorded so you can catch up when work intervenes.

01 / LEARN TOGETHER

Explore.

Work through an idea with your facilitator and peers, grounded in situations that matter to you.

02 / IN YOUR ROLE

Apply.

Try a purposeful change in an actual conversation, decision, or challenge at work.

03 / WITH YOUR COHORT

Discuss.

Compare experiences, ask honest questions, and get another perspective on what happened.

04 / BUILD THE HABIT

Reflect.

Identify what helped, what needs adjusting, and what you’ll put into practice next.

A few practical details

Before you begin.

What does tuition include?

Tuition is $1,000 per person for the eight-week program. After you attend once, you can attend again as often as you like at no additional cost.

Who is the program for?

Managers, directors, and executives across sectors who want to strengthen how they lead people and work through complex challenges. The program applies to business, nonprofit, public-sector, and other organizational settings.

What if I miss a live session?

Live sessions are recorded. You can review the session before the next meeting and continue practicing with your cohort.

How much time should I set aside?

Plan for a 75-minute live session and a 45-minute cohort meeting each week. Practice activities connect to situations in your current role.

Can leaders from the same organization participate together?

The program can be structured around an organizational cohort, allowing colleagues to apply the learning to shared workplace challenges. Details for organizational inquiries will be posted when enrollment opens.
